Fernando Family Hub

Fernando Family Hub is a private app for one household: the Fernando family. It keeps the family's home, money and documents in one place.

What it does

  • Tracks the household's bank accounts, cards, bills, receipts, savings and budgets.
  • With the owner's permission, reads bills, receipts and bank statements from approved senders in Gmail (read-only) and records them for review.
  • Keeps backups in its own folders in the owner's Google Drive: copies of the household's documents, and an encrypted copy of its database.

Who can use it

Only invited members of the household. There is no public sign-up.
